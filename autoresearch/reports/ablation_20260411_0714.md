### Methodology Notes

- **Walk-forward**: expanding training window, no future data leakage
- **Purge**: 90-day gap between train/val and val/test splits
- **Embargo**: 21-day gap between consecutive fold boundaries
- **Scaler**: StandardScaler fit on training data only
- **Loss**: Huber loss (robust to fat tails)
- **Optimizer**: AdamW with cosine annealing + gradient clipping
- **Early stopping**: patience = 3 epochs
- **Strategy**: sign(predicted_return) * actual_return (directional trading)
- **Metrics**: Lopez de Prado (2018) — PSR, DSR, IC, CVaR
