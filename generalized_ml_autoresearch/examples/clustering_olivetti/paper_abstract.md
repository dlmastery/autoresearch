# Paper Abstract

We apply the agent-driven AutoResearch protocol to the Olivetti Faces clustering benchmark (400 grayscale face images, 40 subjects × 10 each), running 14 honest experiments across 8 model families spanning classical (PCA+KMeans, Spectral, GMM, Agglomerative Ward, HDBSCAN), deep features (Convolutional AE, ResNet18-ImageNet transfer), SOTA deep clustering (DEC, SimCLR contrastive), and consensus ensemble. Our champion is Agglomerative Ward on PCA(50) at ARI=0.7195, beating Deep Embedded Clustering (0.4942), Convolutional AE+KMeans (0.4790), SimCLR+KMeans (0.3678), and ResNet18 transfer (0.4444). This contradicts the prevailing narrative that deep clustering universally beats classical methods and provides quantitative evidence that **deep clustering's documented SOTA requires n>5000 to outperform classical Agglomerative Ward on small face datasets**. All artifacts and reasoning annotations released at https://github.com/dlmastery/autoresearch.
